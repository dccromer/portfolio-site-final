Twin Peaks: Cerro Torre
ARC 391 — 2025
A multi-part study beginning with elevation and plan analysis of Cerro Torre, progressing through physical contour models, hanging chain models, Rhino 3D modeling, serial sections, and rendered collages. Explores how natural landform geometry can be abstracted, reconstructed, and reimagined through both physical and digital media.

ARC 121 — Spring 2025
A technical section detail of the wall-floor-to-foundation connection of House Husarö, focusing on thermal and moisture performance in a cold Nordic climate. Documents continuous rigid insulation, vapor barriers, drainage mat, and CLT structural floor systems.

Tamedia Office Building
ARC 121 — Spring 2025
My contributions: structural diagrams, exploded axonometric, sustainability analysis
Analysis of Shigeru Ban's 2013 all-timber headquarters in Zurich — assembled without metal fasteners using traditional Japanese joinery. Examines structural engineering, enclosure systems, load distribution, sun path integration, and carbon-neutral performance.

04 — Team of 5
Haus Gables: Climate Analysis
ARC 121 — Spring 2024
My contributions: climate strategy diagrams, passive ventilation analysis
Climate-responsive analysis of Jennifer Bonner's Haus Gables in Atlanta — examining sun exposure, wind patterns, thermal mass, and passive cooling strategies for a humid subtropical climate. Proposes design improvements including movable louvers, operable windows, and enhanced shading.
