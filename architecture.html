ARC 391 — 2025

02 —
Technical Drawing Climate Analysis Building Systems
Tamedia Office Building: Structure, Enclosure + Climate
ARC 121 — Spring 2025
An individual study of Shigeru Ban's all-timber Tamedia headquarters in Zurich, analyzing how the enclosure works with the structure and the climate. Includes a section showing load distribution, wind flow, and summer and winter sun paths; solar studies for December 21 and June 21; and an exploded axonometric of the prefabricated glulam columns, beams, and floor slabs, assembled without steel connectors.

04 — Team of 5
Haus Gables: Climate Analysis
ARC 121 — Spring 2024
My contributions: hand-drawn climate studies (June sun exposure and surface temperatures, January sun position and heat retention, and existing vs. improved design for shading, ventilation, and cooling); contributed to the written analysis
Climate-responsive analysis of Jennifer Bonner's Haus Gables in Atlanta, examining sun exposure, wind patterns, thermal mass, and passive cooling strategies for a humid subtropical climate. Proposes design improvements including movable louvers, operable windows, and enhanced shading.
